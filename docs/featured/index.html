Featured terms, in plain words

Billing and canceling

Plans are month to month and billed by PayPal on the same day each month. Cancel anytime from your PayPal account (Settings → Payments → Automatic payments) or by emailing info@swats.rocks. You stay Featured until the month you paid for ends. We don't refund partial months.

Founding shop price

The first 15 shops to subscribe pay half price for as long as they stay subscribed without a break. If you cancel and come back later, regular prices apply.

What we can't promise

Featured makes your shop more visible in Knewdle NOW; we can't promise a number of customers or sales. Numbers in your monthly report are counted anonymously from the app.

Which shops qualify

Featured is for places that serve ramen. If a listing isn't a ramen spot, we'll remove it from Featured and refund the current month.

Labels and fairness

Featured shops are always marked “Featured”, as advertising rules and Google Play require. People can still see every shop in the regular list.
