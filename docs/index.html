KnewdleNOW-MenuCheck

Knewdle NOW menu check

Knewdle NOW is a free app that helps people find ramen near them. Some restaurants serve great ramen without "ramen" in their name, so once in a while we read the menu on a restaurant's own website to see whether ramen is on it.

What it does

  • Visits only the restaurant's own site (never delivery apps, review sites or social media), at most a few pages, roughly once a month.
  • Always follows robots.txt.
  • Keeps only a fact ("serves ramen", and how many ramen dishes) plus a link to your menu. It never copies menu text, prices or photos.
  • In the app, your restaurant gets a "Ramen on the menu" tag and a link to your own menu.

Opting out

Add this to your website's robots.txt and the menu check will skip your site:

User-agent: KnewdleNOW-MenuCheck
Disallow: /

Or email info@swats.rocks with your website and we'll add it to the skip list.
