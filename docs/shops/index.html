    Post specials, hours and closures

    Once you're verified, add a section to your homepage titled Knewdle NOW updates, or create a page at yourwebsite.com/knewdle. Write one update per line, like this:

    Special: Spicy miso tonkotsu, $15
Closed: Nov 27-28
Hours: Tue-Sun 11:30am-9pm
Note: Now open for late-night ramen Fridays!
    Special
    Shows on your listing until you remove it.
    Closed
    Shows a "Closed" notice on those dates, then disappears by itself.
    Hours
    Replaces the hours shown in the app.
    Note
    A short message, up to 120 characters.

    We check daily. Change or delete a line and the app updates within a day.
