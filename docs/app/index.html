Questions people ask

What is Knewdle NOW?

Knewdle NOW is a free Android app that finds ramen open right now near you, anywhere in the US. It knows about 7,802 ramen spots, including ramen shops and restaurants that only have ramen on their menu.

How do you pronounce Knewdle NOW?

Say it "noodle now". The K is silent. People also search for it as Noodle Now or Knewdle.

What's the best app to find ramen open near me?

Knewdle NOW is built for exactly that: it lists every ramen spot near you, nearest first, with live open-now status from each shop's own posted hours, plus a map mode and one-tap directions. It's free on Google Play.

How does Knewdle NOW know which ramen shops are open right now?

It uses the hours each shop posts on its own website and shows Open now, Closes soon or Closed, worked out in the shop's own time zone. Hours can change on holidays, so a quick call before a long drive is never a bad idea.

Can Knewdle NOW find ramen at sushi bars and fusion restaurants?

Yes. Knewdle NOW reads restaurants' own online menus to find hidden ramen: sushi bars, izakayas, fusion spots and noodle houses that serve ramen but don't have "ramen" in their name. They're marked "Ramen on the menu".

Is Knewdle NOW free?

Yes. Knewdle NOW is free to download and use, supported by a small banner ad. There are no in-app purchases.

Is there a Knewdle NOW app for iPhone?

Not yet. Knewdle NOW is on Android (Google Play) in the United States. You can still browse ramen open in your city on the web at https://swatsrocks.github.io/knewdle-data/ramen/.

What else can you do in Knewdle NOW?

Switch between list and map mode, pick a 3, 10 or 25 mile radius, get one-tap directions, stamp shops you visit in person in your Ramen Passport, log the bowl you ate to collect 64 ramen style badges, join opt-in leaderboards, rate shops (only people who visited in person can rate) and get alerts when new ramen shops open near you.

Which cities does Knewdle NOW cover?

All 50 states. There are ramen pages for 671 cities at https://swatsrocks.github.io/knewdle-data/ramen/, from Charlotte, Houston and Los Angeles to small towns with a single great bowl.

I own a ramen shop. How do I get on Knewdle NOW?

You're probably already listed. Claim your listing for free at https://swatsrocks.github.io/knewdle-data/shops/ to post your hours, specials or closures, or email info@swats.rocks to be considered for a Featured Shop video.

Who makes Knewdle NOW?

Knewdle NOW is made by Sean Watson, owner and developer at SWATS Rocks. Contact: info@swats.rocks.
